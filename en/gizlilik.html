3. Purposes of processing

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Creating, changing and cancelling your appointment and showing your appointment history
  • Sending confirmation, change and reminder notifications
  • Contacting you about your appointment
  • Planning barber calendars and preventing overlaps
  • Offering hair style suggestions at your request
  • Measuring service quality (occupancy, cancellation and no-show rates; non-identifying summary reports)
  • Ensuring information security and preventing misuse
  • Meeting legal obligations and requests of competent authorities
  • Exercising and protecting legal rights in case of disputes

4. Legal grounds and collection method

Your personal data is collected electronically through the app. For appointments entered manually by a barber it may also be collected verbally by phone. It is processed on the following legal grounds under KVKK Art. 5/2:

  • (c) Establishment or performance of a contract: account, appointments and notifications
  • (ç) Compliance with a legal obligation: statutory retention and official requests
  • (e) Establishment, exercise or protection of a right: transaction records and disputes
  • (f) Legitimate interest (without harming your fundamental rights and freedoms): security, calendar planning and summary reports

Processing based on your explicit consent: publishing a portfolio photo in the app and the optional hair style survey. Separate explicit consent is obtained for these two. You can withdraw consent at any time; the photo is then unpublished and your survey answers are deleted.

5. Transfers

Your personal data is transferred only for the purposes above and only as far as necessary, to:

  • Service providers (in Türkiye or abroad):

- Hosting, database and file storage provider: Supabase Inc. — server region: Frankfurt, Germany (EU)

- Notification delivery services: Expo, Apple Push Notification Service, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Notification contents contain no personal data other than service, barber and time.

  • Competent public authorities: where legally authorised.

Transfers abroad are made in line with KVKK Art. 9: standard contract and notification to the Board under KVKK Art. 9.

Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing.

6. Retention period

Your data is kept for as long as the processing purpose continues and for the periods set by the relevant legislation.

  • When you delete your account, your identity and contact data is deleted or anonymised.
  • Records subject to statutory retention are deleted or anonymised at the end of that period: the period set by the relevant legislation (e.g. 10 years for commercial records).

7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

By applying to the Salon you can:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed
  • Request information about it if processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred
  • Request correction if incomplete or inaccurate
  • Request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7
  • Request that corrections and deletions be notified to third parties it was transferred to
  • Object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing

How to apply: Send your request from your registered email address to devransrtl@gmail.com, or in writing to Güllük Caddesi, Üçgen Mahallesi, Arın Apartmanı, No: 89/C, Muratpaşa / Antalya, Türkiye. Your application is completed free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
