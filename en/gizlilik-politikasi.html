Privacy Policy

Last updated: 04.10.2026

At Bekir Uzun Hair Design (“Salon”) we care about your privacy. This policy summarises what information is processed while you use the app and how it is protected. See the Privacy Notice for details.

1. Information we collect

Location, contacts, camera, microphone and payment information are not collected.

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number.
  • Appointment information: selected service, barber, date and time, appointment status.
  • Device information: a notification key (push token), only if you allow notifications.

2. What we use it for

Your information is not used for advertising, profiling or marketing, and is never sold.

  • To create, confirm, change and cancel your appointment.
  • To send appointment confirmation and reminder notifications.
  • To contact you about your appointment when needed.

3. Sharing

Your information is shared only with the infrastructure providers needed to deliver the service: data hosting (Supabase) and notification delivery (Expo, Apple, Google). It may be shared with competent authorities where required by law.

4. Retention and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access control. When your account is deleted, your personal data is deleted or anonymised, except where the law requires it to be kept.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and request deletion of your data. You can delete your account in the app under Profile > Delete account, or via the account deletion page on our website.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; important changes are announced in the app.
