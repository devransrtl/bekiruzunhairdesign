Account deletion

You can delete your Bekir Uzun Hair Design account and personal data in two ways:

1. In the app

Profile → Delete my account → type DELETE to confirm → Delete my account.

2. Without installing the app

Send an email with the subject “Account deletion request” from the email address registered to your account to devransrtl@gmail.com. Your request is completed within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: name, email, phone, device notification keys, style survey answers, sessions.
  • Your upcoming appointments are cancelled and the barber is notified.
  • Transaction records that must be kept by law are separated from your identity (anonymised) and kept.
