FAQ

How do I cancel my appointment?
In the app: My appointments → appointment → Cancel. If less than 1 hour is left before the appointment, please call the salon.

I don’t get reminders.
Turn on Bekir Uzun Hair Design notifications in your phone’s Settings → Notifications. Your appointments are always visible in the app.

I forgot my password.
Tap “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen and a link is sent to your email. Barber accounts: please contact the salon manager.
