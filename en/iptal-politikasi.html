Cancellation and Change Policy

Last updated: 04.10.2026

1. Cancellation

You can cancel your appointment free of charge in the app under My appointments, up to 1 hour before the appointment time.

2. Changing the time or barber

You can change the time or barber of your appointment in the app up to 1 hour before the appointment time. The new time depends on availability.

3. The last hour

When less than 1 hour is left, cancellations and changes cannot be made in the app. Please call the salon.

4. Fees and refunds

Because no payment is taken in the app, there is no fee for cancelling or changing and no refund process.

5. No-shows

Not showing up without notice prevents other customers from using that time. In repeated cases the Salon may restrict new bookings.

6. Changes by the salon

If the Salon has to cancel or change your appointment, you will be notified and offered a suitable new time.
